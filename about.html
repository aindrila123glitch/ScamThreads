How Scam Threads works

We combine Artificial Intelligence, Cybersecurity, and Web3 to identify online scams, impersonated websites, and digital trust vulnerabilities before you become a victim.

The three pillars

  • 1. Artificial Intelligence: Multilingual natural language analysis inspects SMS, WhatsApp, and email lures in English, Hindi, Bengali, and Hinglish. It detects panic language, isolates prompt-injection attacks, and extracts UPI IDs and phone numbers.
  • 2. Cybersecurity & Digital Trust: Inspects the underlying infrastructure of the web: X.509 certificate chains, Certificate Authority trust (including compromised CA incidents like eMudhra), security headers, cookie flags, and redirect chains.
  • 3. Web3 & Cryptographic Proofs: When you export an evidence report, we compute a Merkle tree of findings and record its root on a smart contract registry (Registry.sol). Anyone can verify that an investigation hasn't been altered. No private data or personal messages ever touch the blockchain.
