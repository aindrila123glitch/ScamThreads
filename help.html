I already clicked something

If you clicked a suspicious link, typed a password, or sent money, take these steps immediately. Acting fast significantly reduces losses.

Immediate assistance

National Cybercrime Helpline: Call 1930 (Toll-Free, 24/7 across India)
Official Portal: cybercrime.gov.in

Step-by-step recovery checklist

  1. 1. Freeze financial accounts Call your bank customer care immediately. Ask them to freeze your debit cards, credit cards, UPI handles, and net-banking access.
  2. 2. Report incident to Cybercrime Helpline 1930 Dial 1930 directly or file a complaint on cybercrime.gov.in. Provide the scammer's UPI ID or the 12-digit transaction reference (UTR) if money was transferred.
  3. 3. Change passwords from a different device Use another phone or laptop to reset passwords on your email, net banking, and social accounts. Turn on two-factor authentication.
  4. 4. Remove remote access tools If you were told to install AnyDesk, TeamViewer, RustDesk, or QuickSupport, disconnect from the internet immediately and uninstall the app.
  5. 5. Preserve all evidence Take screenshots of the text messages, WhatsApp chats, scam web address, caller phone number, and payment receipts. Do not delete messages.

What to do based on what you shared

  • Entered an OTP: The scammer likely attempted an immediate bank transfer. Call your bank immediately to block the transfer.
  • Entered a password: Change that password immediately on your real account, plus any other accounts where you used the same password.
  • Installed an app (AnyDesk, TeamViewer): The scammer can control your screen. Turn off Wi-Fi/mobile data right now and uninstall the app.
  • Sent money via UPI / QR: Note down the 12-digit UTR number from your payment app and report it to 1930 within the golden hour.
